## この記入テンプレートの使い方 / How to Fill This Template

1. **Score honestly**: The value of this checklist depends entirely on honesty. If you inflate scores, you are only hurting yourself.
2. **Fill with a partner**: Have someone else review your scores. Solo evaluations tend to be biased upward.
3. **Use the Sunk Cost Warning seriously**: Read it out loud before making your final decision.
4. **Document the KILL learnings**: If you kill an initiative, the "what we take with us" section is the most valuable part -- it ensures the investment was not wasted.
5. **Set a review date for GO/PIVOT**: A GO decision is not permanent. Set a date to re-evaluate.
6. **Run this quarterly**: Even for initiatives that are going well, a quarterly Go/No-Go keeps you honest.
