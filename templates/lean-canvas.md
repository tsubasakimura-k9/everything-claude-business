# Lean Canvas / リーンキャンバス

> One-page business model snapshot based on Ash Maurya's Lean Canvas format.
> Fill in each block with your current best understanding. Mark each entry with status markers to track what needs validation.

**Status Markers / ステータスマーカー:**
- `[A]` = Assumption (未検証の仮定)
- `[E]` = Evidence-based (エビデンスあり)
- `[?]` = Unknown (不明 -- 調査が必要)

## Block Instructions / 各ブロックの記入ガイド

### 1. 課題 / Problem (Top 3 Problems)
List the top 1-3 problems your customer segment faces. Be specific and observable.
- What pain are they experiencing today?
- How are they currently solving it? (Existing Alternatives)
- **Good**: "Sales teams spend 4+ hours/week manually updating CRM records"
- **Bad**: "CRM is hard to use"
- **Typically starts as**: [A]ssumption until customer interviews confirm

### 2. 顧客セグメント / Customer Segments (Target Users)
Who specifically has this problem? Be as narrow as possible initially.
- Define your early adopter, not the mainstream market
- Include demographics, psychographics, and context
- **Good**: "B2B SaaS founders with 10-50 employees, pre-Series A, who do their own sales"
- **Bad**: "Small businesses"
- **Typically starts as**: [A]ssumption, becomes [E]vidence after interviews

### 3. 独自の価値提案 / Unique Value Proposition (Single Clear Message)
One sentence that explains why you are different and worth paying attention to.
- Focus on the end result, not features
- Should be understandable in 5 seconds
- **Good**: "Get your CRM updated without touching your CRM"
- **Bad**: "AI-powered CRM automation platform"
- **Typically starts as**: [A]ssumption, validated through landing page tests

### 4. ソリューション / Solution (Top 3 Features)
List the simplest possible features that address the top problems.
- Map each solution to a specific problem
- Think MVP, not final product
- Resist listing more than 3
- **Typically starts as**: [A]ssumption until you build and test

### 5. チャネル / Channels (Path to Customer)
How will you reach your customer segments?
- Free vs paid channels
- Inbound vs outbound
- Consider the full funnel: awareness, evaluation, purchase, delivery, referral
- **Typically starts as**: [A]ssumption, becomes [E]vidence after first acquisition experiments

### 6. 収益の流れ / Revenue Streams (Revenue Model)
How will you make money?
- Pricing model (subscription, usage, one-time, freemium)
- Price point and basis for it
- Revenue per customer estimate
- **Typically starts as**: [A]ssumption until first paying customers

### 7. コスト構造 / Cost Structure (Fixed and Variable Costs)
What are the major costs to operate?
- Customer acquisition cost
- Hosting/infrastructure
- Personnel
- List both fixed and variable costs
- **Typically starts as**: Mix of [A]ssumption and [E]vidence

### 8. 主要指標 / Key Metrics (Activities You Measure)
What numbers tell you the business is healthy?
- Pirate Metrics: Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Revenue, Referral (AARRR)
- Pick 1-2 that matter most at your current stage
- **Typically starts as**: [A]ssumption, refined through operation

### 9. 圧倒的な優位性 / Unfair Advantage (Cannot Be Easily Copied or Bought)
What makes you defensible in the long run?
- This is the hardest box to fill honestly
- Examples: insider information, expert endorsements, existing audience, network effects, community, proprietary data
- "First mover" and "passion" are NOT unfair advantages
- It is OK to leave this blank initially
- **Typically starts as**: [A]ssumption, often remains one

## 検証優先順位 / Validation Priority

Fill the canvas in this recommended order (riskiest assumptions first):

1. **課題 / Problem** -- Does this problem actually exist?
2. **顧客セグメント / Customer Segments** -- Who has this problem most acutely?
3. **独自の価値提案 / Unique Value Proposition** -- Does our framing resonate?
4. **チャネル / Channels** -- Can we reach them?
5. **収益の流れ / Revenue Streams** -- Will they pay?
6. **ソリューション / Solution** -- Does our approach work?
7. **主要指標 / Key Metrics** -- Are we measuring the right things?
8. **コスト構造 / Cost Structure** -- Can we deliver profitably?
9. **圧倒的な優位性 / Unfair Advantage** -- What will keep us ahead?

---

## レビューチェックリスト / Review Checklist

- [ ] Every block marked as [A] has a corresponding experiment planned
- [ ] Problem and Customer Segments are validated with real interviews (not just desk research)
- [ ] Solution maps directly to stated problems (no feature creep)
- [ ] Revenue model has been tested (even informally: "Would you pay $X for this?")
- [ ] Canvas reviewed and updated within the last 2 weeks

## 記入例 / Filled Example

> Scenario: AI-powered receipt scanning for Japanese SMB accounting (中小企業向けAI領収書スキャン)

| Block | Content | Status |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **課題 / Problem** | 1. 経理担当が月40時間を手入力に費やしている 2. 手入力ミスによる税務リスク 3. インボイス制度対応で記帳項目が増加 | [E] -- 12社インタビュー済 |
| **顧客セグメント / Customer Segments** | 従業員10-50名の中小企業、経理専任1-2名、弥生/freee利用中 | [E] -- 8社が該当 |
| **独自の価値提案 / Unique Value Proposition** | 「領収書を撮るだけ。仕訳も、インボイス番号チェックも、全部自動。」 | [A] -- LP未テスト |
| **ソリューション / Solution** | 1. スマホ撮影→OCR→自動仕訳 2. インボイス番号の国税庁DB自動照合 3. 弥生/freee自動連携 | [A] -- プロトタイプ未作成 |
| **チャネル / Channels** | 税理士パートナー経由の紹介、IT導入補助金対象として申請支援とセット | [A] -- 税理士2名に打診中 |
| **収益の流れ / Revenue Streams** | 月額9,800円/社（50枚まで）、追加100円/枚。年契約で2ヶ月無料 | [A] -- 5社に価格提示予定 |
| **コスト構造 / Cost Structure** | OCR API: ~2円/枚、サーバー: 月5万、開発人件費: 月80万 | [E]/[A] 混合 |
| **主要指標 / Key Metrics** | アクティベーション率（初回アップロード完了）、月次継続率 | [A] -- 運用開始後に計測 |
| **圧倒的な優位性 / Unfair Advantage** | インボイス制度特化の学習データ蓄積、税理士ネットワーク | [A] |

## この記入テンプレートの使い方 / How to Fill This Template

1. **Start rough**: Fill every block with your best guess. Mark everything as [A].
2. **Prioritize validation**: Use the validation priority order above to decide what to test first.
3. **Update frequently**: Revisit the canvas every 1-2 weeks as you gather evidence.
4. **Move markers**: As you validate, change [A] to [E] and note the evidence source.
5. **Use with experiments**: For each [A] block, create an experiment card (`templates/experiment-card.md`) to design a test.
6. **Keep it one page**: If you need more space, your blocks are too detailed -- save details for supporting documents.
