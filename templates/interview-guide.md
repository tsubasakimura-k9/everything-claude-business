# Customer Interview Guide / 顧客インタビューガイド

> Extract facts about the past, not opinions about the future. Based on "The Mom Test" by Rob Fitzpatrick.
> Core rule: Talk about their life, not your idea.

## インタビュー構成 / Interview Structure

### Phase 1: 導入 / Opening (2-3 minutes)

Set context. Build rapport. Do NOT pitch.

**Script:**
> "Thanks for taking the time. I'm trying to understand how [general topic area] works for people like you. I'd love to hear about your experience. There are no right or wrong answers -- I'm just trying to learn."

**日本語スクリプト:**
> 「お時間いただきありがとうございます。[一般的なテーマ]について、実際のご経験をお聞きしたいと思っています。正解・不正解はありませんので、率直なお話を聞かせてください。」

**Rules:**
- Do NOT describe your product or idea
- Do NOT say "I'm building..." or "We're thinking about..."
- DO frame it as learning, not selling

---

### Phase 2: 核心質問 / Core Questions -- Their Life, Not Your Idea (15-20 minutes)

#### Understanding Their World

| DO (The Mom Test) | DON'T |
|---|---|
| "Tell me about how you currently handle [process]." | "Would you use a tool that does X?" |
| "What's the hardest part about [doing thing]?" | "Don't you think X is a problem?" |
| "Walk me through the last time you [did thing]." | "How much would you pay for X?" |
| "Why was that hard?" | "Do you like the idea of X?" |
| "What have you tried to solve this?" | "Would you recommend X to a friend?" |
| "What happened after that?" | "On a scale of 1-10, how painful is X?" |

#### 推奨質問フロー / Recommended Question Flow

**1. 現状把握 / Context / Status Quo**
- "Tell me about how you currently [do the relevant activity]."
- "What does a typical [day/week/process] look like for you regarding [topic]?"
- 日本語: 「[テーマ]について、普段どのようにされていますか？」

**2. 課題発見 / Problem Discovery**
- "What's the hardest part about [that process]?"
- "Why is that hard?" (Follow up -- dig deeper)
- "What else have you tried?" (Reveals competitive landscape)
- 日本語: 「その中で一番大変なのはどの部分ですか？」

**3. 具体的エピソード / Specifics / Last Time (Most Important)**
- "Can you walk me through the last time that happened?"
- "When was the last time you [experienced the problem]?"
- "What did you do about it?"
- "How much time/money did that cost you?"
- 日本語: 「直近でそれが起きたとき、具体的にどう対応されましたか？」

> The "last time" question is the most powerful tool you have. It forces specifics instead of generalities. If they cannot recall a specific instance, the problem may not be real or frequent enough.

**4. 既存ソリューション / Existing Solutions / Workarounds**
- "How are you solving this today?"
- "What do you like about your current approach?"
- "What do you wish was different?"
- "Have you looked for a better solution? What happened?"
- 日本語: 「今はどうやって対応されていますか？」

**5. 感情的な重み / Emotional Weight**
- "How does that make you feel when [problem occurs]?"
- "If you could wave a magic wand, what would change?"
- "Is this something you've complained about to colleagues?"
- 日本語: 「もし魔法の杖があったら、何を変えたいですか？」

---

### Phase 3: 深掘り / Specific Deep-Dives (5-10 minutes)

Pick 1-2 areas from the core questions that revealed energy/pain and go deeper.

**Follow-up patterns:**
- "You mentioned [X]. Tell me more about that."
- "You said [X] was frustrating. What specifically was frustrating about it?"
- "You tried [solution]. Why did you stop using it?"
- "How much are you currently spending on [current solution]?"

---

### Phase 4: コミットメント / Commitment / Advancement (3-5 minutes)

> If you leave a meeting without a commitment, the meeting was worthless. -- Rob Fitzpatrick

Ask for something concrete. The strength of their commitment reveals the truth of their pain.

**Escalating commitments (weakest to strongest):**

| Level | Ask | Signal |
|-------|-----|--------|
| Time | "Can I follow up with you in 2 weeks to share what I learn?" | Weak interest |
| Reputation | "Is there someone else who has this problem I should talk to?" | Moderate interest |
| Effort | "Would you be willing to try a prototype and give feedback?" | Strong interest |
| Money | "Would you pre-order / pay for early access at $X?" | Very strong signal |
| Integration | "Can we do a pilot with your team?" | Strongest signal |

**Script:**
> "This has been really helpful. Based on what you've shared, we're exploring some solutions in this area. [Choose appropriate ask from above]. Would that be possible?"

**日本語スクリプト:**
> 「大変参考になりました。お話を踏まえて、この領域でソリューションを検討しています。[適切な依頼]...ということは可能でしょうか？」

---

### Phase 5: 締め / Wrap-Up (1-2 minutes)

- "Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?"
- "Is there anyone else you'd recommend I talk to about this?"
- Thank them genuinely.
- 日本語: 「他にお聞きすべきだったことはありますか？ 同じテーマでお話を伺える方をご存知でしたらご紹介いただけると助かります。」

## よくある失敗チェックリスト / Common Mistakes Checklist

Before each interview, review:

- [ ] I will NOT pitch my idea
- [ ] I will NOT ask "would you use X?"
- [ ] I will NOT ask about the future ("would you...?") -- I will ask about the past ("last time you...")
- [ ] I will NOT fish for compliments
- [ ] I WILL ask for specific stories and examples
- [ ] I WILL follow up on vague answers with "can you give me an example?"
- [ ] I WILL ask for a commitment at the end
- [ ] I WILL shut up and listen (aim for 80% them, 20% me)

## 記入例 / Filled Example

> Scenario: Interviewing a SMB accountant about receipt processing pain

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **対象者** | A氏（匿名） |
| **役職** | 経理主任 |
| **会社** | 製造業、従業員35名 |
| **日付** | 2026-01-20 |
| **インタビュアー** | 木村 |
| **インタビュー番号** | 3 of 10 |
| **所要時間** | 35分 |
| **チャネル** | Video (Zoom) |

**知りたいこと:**
1. 月次の経費精算にどれくらい時間がかかっているか
2. インボイス制度対応でどう変わったか
3. 既存ツール（freee）のどこに不満があるか

**テストする仮定:** 「経理担当は月20時間以上を領収書の手入力に費やしている」

**重要な引用:**
1. > 「月末は毎回3日くらい残業して領収書を打ち込んでます。営業が50人分持ってくるので」
2. > 「freeeのOCR試したんですけど、読み取り精度が悪くて結局手で直すから二度手間なんです」
3. > 「インボイス番号のチェックが増えて、去年の1.5倍くらい時間かかってます」

**課題は確認できたか:** Yes [E]
- 月末に約30時間を経費精算に充てている。本人の見積もりではなく、勤怠記録で確認済。

**課題の深刻度:** [x] They have cobbled together a workaround (freee OCR + 手修正)

**行動意欲:** [x] Gave time or introduction -- 同業の経理担当2名を紹介してくれた

**仮定と矛盾した点:** freeeのOCR機能は「知っているが使い物にならない」という評価。既存ツールの精度が競合ではなく追い風になる可能性。

## この記入テンプレートの使い方 / How to Fill This Template

1. **Prepare before the interview**: Fill in the metadata, learning goals, and riskiest assumption BEFORE the call.
2. **Print the Common Mistakes Checklist**: Review it right before each interview as a pre-flight check.
3. **Take raw notes during**: Focus on exact quotes and specific facts. Do not interpret during the interview.
4. **Fill interpretation within 24 hours**: Memory fades fast. The interpretation section should be completed the same day.
5. **Synthesize every 5 interviews**: After every 5 interviews, review all notes together to find patterns.
6. **Track commitments**: If they offered an introduction or agreed to test a prototype, follow up within 48 hours.
